How The Agent Works

  1. Reads and validates every row in your Excel or CSV sheet.
  2. Combines global context with row-level tone, language, length, and override prompt.
  3. Generates a personalized HTML email body with local models or free-tier AI providers.
  4. Pauses for human review in the editor before sending.
  5. Adds attachments, sends through SMTP, or creates a local `.eml` draft.
Zero-cost mode is enabled from .env. Use AI_PROVIDER=template, ollama, lmstudio, groq, gemini, or local openai_compatible. Row prompts are off by default to avoid accidental sample-data contamination.
